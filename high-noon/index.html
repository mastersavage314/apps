              How Gaia keeps time

              One day36hoursSelene's tides slowed the planet's spin.
              On the dial18numbersThe hour hand goes around twice a day.
              One week5daysMonday, Thursday, Friday, Saturday, Sunday.
              One year243daysAnd 244 in even-numbered years.
              Time zones36zonesEach one hour wide, counted from Summitbrook.
              Last clock to first44hoursFrom Baker Island to Hitirā.
              Daylight saving jump1hourThe same as on Earth.
              The standardZTCCoordinated Zenith Time, set from Summitbrook.

              Gaian clocks run in real time with the same hours, minutes and seconds as Earth's. Gaia's year 2026 began at the same moment Summitbrook's did on Earth, and every Gaian day since has lasted 36 hours, so the dates on the two planets do not line up.

              Every Gaian offset comes from its Earth one: add 9 hours, multiply by 1.5, and settle on a whole hour, with any odd minutes carried over. Places with clock changes follow the US or European dates. Parinacota is shown on Chile's standard time all year, since Gaia's clock-change dates for the southern half of the planet are not locked yet.
